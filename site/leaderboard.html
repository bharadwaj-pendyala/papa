Which open-source docs are easiest to read?

Papa graded the docs of 14 projects. The grade is a US school grade: under 10 reads easily. Lower is better.

Projectgrade 10

    How the grade is measured

    • Each file's grade is the mean of three formulas: ARI, Flesch-Kincaid, and Gunning fog. A project's grade is the mean of its files, weighted by sentences.
    • Papa grades prose only. It skips code blocks, inline code, JSX and HTML tags, MDX expressions, Hugo shortcodes, link URLs, and front matter.
    • A hard sentence reads at grade 10 or above, and a very hard one at grade 14 or above. Papa does not judge accuracy, structure, or examples, and short API reference pages can score high on long technical words alone.
    • Each project links to the folder and commit that papa graded.

    Run it on your own docs: pipx run --spec papa-lint papa docs/, or paste a page into the playground.

    Grades come from papa, an open-source readability linter. A score is a prompt to look, not a verdict on a project.
